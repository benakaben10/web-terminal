# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualised language, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages and any other project
space, and to anyone taking part.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or contacting the
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions that break these rules, and may block a participant who
keeps breaking them.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately.
